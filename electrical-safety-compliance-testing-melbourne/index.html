Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers from our licensed Melbourne electricians.

Q: What happens if a rental property fails an electrical safety check?

If safety defects (such as damaged power points or missing RCD protection) are identified, we document the issues clearly with photo evidence and provide an itemized quote to perform urgent remediation on the spot.

Q: Who can conduct an official Victorian electrical safety audit?

Only a licensed electrician holding an active A-Grade Electrical Licence registered in Victoria can conduct and sign off on compliant safety checks under Energy Safe Victoria guidelines.

Q: Do you provide documentation directly to real estate property managers?

Yes. We supply comprehensive digital compliance certificates and invoices directly to real estate property management portals for streamlined record keeping.
