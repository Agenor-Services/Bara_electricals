Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers from our licensed Melbourne electricians.

Q: Can smart automation be installed in an existing home without tearing down walls?

Yes! We utilize modern smart micro-modules and wireless zigbee/mesh switches that fit directly behind your existing switch plates without plaster damage.

Q: Do smart switches require a neutral wire?

Most premium smart switches require a neutral wire for continuous standby power. If your existing switch loops lack a neutral, our licensed sparkies can run a neutral feed or install compatible bypass modules.

Q: What happens to my lights if the internet goes down?

All systems we install retain full physical switch functionality. Even during an internet outage, your light switches and safety controls continue working normally like traditional switches.
