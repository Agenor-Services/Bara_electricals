## Unlock Fast, Safe Home Charging for Your Electric Vehicle

Relying on public charging stations or slow 10-amp trickle plugs is inconvenient and inefficient. A dedicated Level 2 EV charging station installed in your garage or driveway allows you to recharge overnight using off-peak electricity tariffs.

### Supported Vehicles & Charging Units
We install all leading Australian-approved charging systems:
- **Tesla Wall Connector (Gen 3):** Seamless integration with Tesla Model 3, Model Y, Model S, and Model X.
- **Universal Type 2 Charging Units:** Ocular, Schneider EVlink, Zappi, Autel, and Fronius chargers compatible with BYD, Hyundai, Kia, MG, Polestar, and Volvo.
- **Solar-Integrated Smart Chargers:** Divert excess rooftop solar generation directly into your car battery rather than feeding the grid for low feed-in tariffs.

### Safe Installation Standards
Under AS/NZS 3000 and AS/NZS IEC 61851, EV chargers require:
- A dedicated radial circuit from the main switchboard.
- Type A or Type B RCD protection designed for DC fault current detection.
- Appropriately derated 6mm² or 10mm² heavy copper conductors to withstand continuous 32A thermal loads.
