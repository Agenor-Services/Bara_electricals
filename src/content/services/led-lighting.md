## Transform Your Living Spaces with Modern LED Technology

Lighting sets the character of your home while representing a significant portion of quarterly electricity bills. Upgrading from 50W halogens to 8W-10W LED fixtures delivers immediate cost savings and superior visual comfort.

### Residential & Commercial Lighting Solutions
- **IC-4 Fire-Rated Downlights:** Sealed units that prevent dust, draughts, and insects from entering ceiling voids while maintaining roof insulation continuity.
- **Architectural Pendant & Feature Lighting:** Secure mounting and precision wiring for kitchen island pendants, dining room chandeliers, and vaulted ceilings.
- **Smart Lighting Controls:** App-controlled and voice-activated dimmers (Clipsal Wiser, Caseta, and Zigbee) for automated scheduling.
- **Security & Garden Floodlights:** Motion-activated sensor lights, weatherproof garden spikes, and low-voltage deck step lights.
