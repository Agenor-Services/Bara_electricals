## Precision Electrical Integration for Renovations & Extensions

Renovating a home is the ideal time to modernize your electrical infrastructure before plaster and tiles are sealed. Whether updating an older brick home in Sunshine or extending a family residence in Caroline Springs, we coordinate directly with your builders and cabinetmakers.

### Complete House Rewiring Services
- **Elimination of Hazardous Wiring:** Removal of Vulcanised Indian Rubber (VIR), split-metal conduit, and ungrounded circuits.
- **High-Capacity Kitchen & Bath Circuits:** Induction cooktop circuits, under-cabinet LED task lighting, pop-up island power points, and 3-in-1 bathroom heater/exhaust units.
- **Sub-Board Installations:** Dedicated sub-switchboards for detached studios, outdoor entertainment pavilions, and garages.
- **Outdoor Entertaining & Alfresco Power:** Weatherproof outdoor power points, infrared patio heaters, garden spotlighting, and pool pump connections.
