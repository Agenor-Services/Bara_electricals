## Why Melbourne Homes Need Modern Switchboards

Many established homes across Burnside, Sunshine, Deer Park, and Melton were constructed with ceramic fuse switchboards designed for 1970s electrical loads — long before the era of split-system air conditioning, solar inverters, induction cooktops, and electric vehicles.

Old ceramic fuse holders present three critical dangers:

1. **Fire Hazard from Incorrect Fuse Wire:** When a fuse blows, homeowners often replace the element with thicker wire or foil. This allows circuits to overheat inside wall cavities, causing structural timber fires.
2. **Zero Electrocution Protection:** Ceramic fuses only protect cables against massive overcurrent; they do not detect earth leakage. A person can receive a fatal 240V shock without a fuse blowing.
3. **Absence of Arc and Surge Protection:** Modern electronics, smart televisions, and heat pumps are vulnerable to power grid voltage spikes without dedicated Type 2 surge diverters installed at the switchboard.
