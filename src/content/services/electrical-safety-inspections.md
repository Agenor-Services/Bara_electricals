## Ensure Total Legal Compliance & Family Safety

Electrical faults cause hundreds of house fires in Victoria each year. A comprehensive electrical safety inspection detects dangerous insulation breakdown, ungrounded metalwork, and overloaded circuits before catastrophic failures occur.

### The Comprehensive Safety Audit Checklist
1. **Switchboard & Earthing Audit:** Verification of main earth stake resistance, neutral link integrity, and RCD trip timing.
2. **Polarity & Earth Continuity Testing:** Testing all domestic power points for correct active/neutral polarity and effective earthing.
3. **Smoke Alarm Compliance:** Inspection of hardwired interconnected smoke alarms to verify compliance with AS 3786.
4. **Appliance & Cooktop Inspection:** Checking stationary electrical equipment (ovens, rangehoods, water heaters) for physical damage or loose terminals.
5. **Formal Certification:** Issuing an official Victorian Compliance Record and Certificate of Electrical Safety (CES).
