## Dependable Commercial Electrical Services That Keep Your Business Running

Commercial premises demand robust electrical infrastructure that withstands intensive operational loads. Downtime means lost revenue — our experienced team responds quickly to commercial faults and delivers scheduled maintenance that fits around your business operations.

### Core Commercial Capabilities
- **Commercial Switchboards & Distribution Boards:** Three-phase power distribution, sub-metering, and energy management.
- **Office, Retail & Hospitality Fitouts:** Data cabling (Cat6/Cat6A), floor boxes, feature lighting, and commercial kitchen connections.
- **Statutory Compliance Maintenance:** Routine testing of emergency lighting (AS 2293) and RCD trip testing.
- **Security & Access Control:** Commercial CCTV networks, keyless entry systems, and high-bay warehouse LED lighting.
