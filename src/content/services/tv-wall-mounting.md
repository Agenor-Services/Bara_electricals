## Achieve a Clean, Floating Entertainment Display

A poorly mounted television is both a visual distraction and a serious tip-over hazard for young children. Our certified electricians securely mount screens of all sizes, ensuring perfectly level viewing angles and completely concealed wiring.

### What is Included in Our TV Mounting Service
- **Precision Stud Location & Anchor Installation:** Fastening into structural wall studs to hold displays up to 100kg.
- **In-Wall Power Point Relocation:** Legally installing a dedicated 240V power outlet behind the TV (extension leads must not be hidden inside wall cavities under AS/NZS 3000).
- **Brushed Wall Plates for AV Cabling:** Clean entry and exit brush plates for multiple 4K/120Hz HDMI cables, gaming consoles, and soundbars.
- **Soundbar & Speaker Mounting:** Alignment and secure mounting of matching soundbars directly beneath the display.
