## The Life-Saving Difference of an RCD

A standard circuit breaker or fuse only cuts power when high currents (e.g. 16 to 32 Amps) flow during a massive short circuit. However, it takes as little as **50 milliamperes (0.05 Amps)** passing through a human chest to cause ventricular fibrillation and death.

A Residual Current Device (RCD):
- Continuously monitors active and neutral currents.
- Detects any current imbalance exceeding 30mA.
- Cuts electricity in under 300 milliseconds — faster than the blink of an eye.
