An electrical emergency is an active situation involving visible sparks, burning plastic or fishy smells, sizzling or buzzing switchboard components, water contact with live power, or electric shock. If you detect any of these signs, immediately isolate the power at the main switch if safe to do so from a dry vantage point, evacuate the immediate hazard area, and call an emergency licensed electrician immediately on 0403 669 041.

## Recognizing Severe Electrical Emergencies

Electrical hazards can rapidly escalate into structural fires or fatal electrocution. Treat the following scenarios as top-priority emergencies:

1. **Burning Fish or Plastic Smells from Outlets:** Indicates melting polycarbonate plastics and continuous arcing inside the wall cavity.
2. **Switchboard Buzzing, Sizzling, or Radiating Heat:** Indicates high-resistance loose connections or imminent insulation breakdown on the main incoming supply.
3. **Repeated Safety Switch Trips That Won't Reset:** Indicates an active dead short or low-resistance earth fault that requires professional fault-isolation.
4. **Water Ingress into Electrical Fittings:** Storm roof leaks or burst plumbing entering downlights or power points.
5. **Electric Tingles from Taps or Appliances:** Indicates a broken main neutral wire or failed earthing system, causing metallic plumbing to become energised.

## When to Call 000 vs an Emergency Electrician

If an active open flame is visible, smoke is filling your home, or someone has received a severe electric shock and is unresponsive, call **000 for the Country Fire Authority (CFA) / Fire Rescue Victoria (FRV) or Ambulance Victoria immediately**. 

Once immediate physical danger is contained, or if the hazard is contained without active open fire (such as a smouldering outlet or isolated tripped switchboard), call our licensed emergency electrician on **0403 669 041** to isolate, diagnose, and legally recertify the system before re-energisation.
