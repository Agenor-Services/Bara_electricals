## The Hidden Dangers of Ceramic Fuses

In the 1960s and 70s across Melbourne's West, ceramic fuses were standard trade practice. Today, they represent the single biggest electrical safety hazard in established housing.

### Why You Should Upgrade Today:
1. **Immediate RCD Protection:** Upgrading to modern RCBOs guarantees power cuts in under 300 milliseconds if an appliance leaks current.
2. **No More Fiddling with Fuse Wire in the Dark:** If a circuit trips, you simply flip a toggle switch up rather than hunting for tools and wire in the dark.
3. **Surge Protection:** Protect thousands of dollars in home computers, smart TVs, and appliances with integrated Type 2 surge diverters.
4. **Compliance for Renting or Selling:** Modern safety switches are mandatory for all rental properties in Victoria under the Residential Tenancies Act.
