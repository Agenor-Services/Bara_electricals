## How Power Point Circuits Operate

In Australian domestic installations, power outlets are wired using 2.5mm² TPS cable connected in a radial or ring sub-circuit protected by a 16A or 20A circuit breaker.

Because power points are connected sequentially, a fault at an early socket (such as in the hallway) can cause sockets downstream in the living room and bedrooms to lose power simultaneously.

Our electricians quickly test each node in the chain to locate open circuits, replace failed switch mechanisms, and restore full household power with compliant Clipsal or Hager faceplates.
