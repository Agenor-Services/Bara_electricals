## Diagnosing the Scope of the Flicker

To pinpoint the fault, determine whether the issue is **localised** (one globe), **circuit-wide** (one room or group of lights), or **whole-house**:

- **Single Fitting:** Loose globe, dying LED driver, or a worn lamp holder.
- **Single Room / Circuit:** Incompatible dimmer mechanism, overloaded circuit, or loose junction box in ceiling.
- **Entire House:** Loose neutral connection at switchboard or incoming street mains connection problem.

Our electricians use digital oscilloscopes and true-RMS multimeters to measure supply voltage stability under load and rectify the root cause safely.
