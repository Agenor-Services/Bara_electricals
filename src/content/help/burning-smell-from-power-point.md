## What To Do in the First 60 Seconds

A burning smell from an electrical outlet is one of the most critical warning signs in any home. Electrical fires frequently smolder inside wall cavities unnoticed before breaking through into living spaces.

### Immediate Action Plan:
1. **Isolate Power at the Switchboard:** Do not waste time trying to unplug the appliance. Go straight to your main switchboard and turn off the breaker for that circuit.
2. **Keep Clear:** Keep children and pets away from the room.
3. **Check for Thermal Spread:** If the plasterboard surrounding the switch is hot to the touch or discolored, alert household members.
4. **Call a Licensed Emergency Electrician:** Have the socket and hidden cables physically inspected and replaced before restoring power.
