## Deciphering Smoke Alarm Sounds

- **One Chirp Every 60 Seconds:** Low battery warning or sensor chamber contamination.
- **Continuous Loud Blaring Siren:** Active smoke or combustion particles detected.
- **Three Quick Beeps in Succession:** Hardware sensor failure indicating the unit must be replaced.

In Victoria, photoelectric smoke alarms are strongly recommended over outdated ionization models because they respond significantly faster to smoldering furniture fires typical of domestic living spaces.
