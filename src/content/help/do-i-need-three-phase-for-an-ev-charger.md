## Single-Phase vs Three-Phase EV Charging Comparison

| Feature | Single-Phase (7.4 kW) | Three-Phase (11 kW – 22 kW) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Voltage & Current** | 230V / 32 Amps | 400V / 16A to 32A per phase |
| **Range Added Per Hour** | 40 to 50 km/hr | 75 to 120 km/hr |
| **Full Charge Time (60kWh)** | 7 to 8 hours (Overnight) | 3 to 5 hours |
| **Grid Supply Cost** | $0 (Uses existing supply) | $2,500 – $6,000+ (Distributor upgrade) |
| **Installation Complexity** | Straightforward | Requires 3-phase switchboard |

For the vast majority of suburban Melbourne households, **a 7kW single-phase installation is the sweet spot**: it completely recharges your vehicle while you sleep, costs significantly less to install, and avoids expensive network supply upgrade fees.
