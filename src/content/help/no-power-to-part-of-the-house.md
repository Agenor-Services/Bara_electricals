## Understanding Partial Outages

When only part of your home loses electricity, your electrical installation is actively isolating a fault to protect the rest of the property.

### Common Scenarios:
- **Only Lighting Out:** Lighting circuit RCBO has tripped, commonly caused by a blown globe creating an internal short or water in an exterior spotlight.
- **Only Power Points Out:** Power sub-circuit has tripped due to an overloaded heater or faulty appliance.
- **Multiple Rooms on One Side Out:** Typical of three-phase properties where Phase 1 or Phase 2 has disconnected.
