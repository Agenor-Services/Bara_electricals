Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers from our licensed Melbourne electricians.

Q: How do I know if my switchboard is outdated or dangerous?

Warning signs include ceramic wire-wound fuses, flickering lights, circuit breakers that frequently trip, burning smells near the meter box, or buzzing sounds. Any switchboard over 25 years old should be inspected.

Q: How long will my power be turned off during a switchboard upgrade?

Power is typically disconnected for approximately 3 to 5 hours while our licensed electricians replace the enclosure, install new RCBOs, and run comprehensive circuit polarity and insulation resistance tests.

Q: Does a switchboard upgrade help with rental property compliance?

Yes. Under Victorian rental minimum standards, all rental properties must have modern circuit breakers and safety switches installed for both power and lighting circuits.
